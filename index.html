Incognito Block – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 nov 2026

Incognito Block ("the app") is published by Safe Labs. Contact: awais9027prince@gmail.com.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your device

  • A salted hash of your password and of your recovery answer, plus your recovery question text, so the app can check your password. The password itself is never stored.
  • Your settings (which switches are on) and a counter of how many times a blocked incognito button was tapped.

This information never leaves your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Android backup is switched off for the app.

Accessibility Service

With your explicit consent, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service for two purposes only: (1) to find the "New Incognito tab" and similar private-tab buttons in supported web browsers, by matching their on-screen labels, and cover them so they cannot be tapped (if a private tab is started anyway, the app returns to the Home screen and closes the browser); (2) to detect when Android Settings or the app installer shows a screen that could switch off or remove the app, so it can ask for your password first. The service looks only at supported browsers and at Settings and installer screens. It does not read web page content, passwords or messages. Screen content is examined in memory and immediately discarded; it is never saved or sent anywhere.

Device Administrator

The app asks to be a device administrator so that it cannot be uninstalled without your password. The only policy it requests is "force lock", used to lock the screen after repeated wrong passwords. No data is collected through this permission.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
